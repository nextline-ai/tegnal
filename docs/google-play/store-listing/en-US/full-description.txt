Find works you love. Keep them organized.

TEGNAL helps you browse images and comics from compatible websites you choose to connect. Search by tag, save your favorites, and build a library of your own.

Folders that feel like yours
Choose folder names and colors, arrange them in your preferred order, and recognize collections by their previews. Select several works to organize them together. Image Mode offers site-based default folders alongside your own folders.

Searches worth keeping
Find tags with input suggestions and use the newest or popular sorting options supported by each site. Save tag combinations as searches and exclude tags you do not want to see.

A focused viewer
View images, animated GIFs and videos. Read comics with horizontal or vertical paging, continuous scrolling, zoom and resume. Keep notes on works, or download supported works separately to open them offline.

Two library modes
Switch between Image Mode and Comics Mode at the top of the screen. Keep their libraries and preferences separate, and explore artists, tags and image collections provided by connected sites.

Import data from other manga viewers
Choose a supported SQLite database, preview item counts and merge it into your existing library. Other backup formats are available in Settings. You can also export and restore TEGNAL JSON backups. Import coverage varies by format. JSON backups exclude media files and some original source records.

Fill missing comic information after import
After a successful import, TEGNAL automatically requests missing comic covers, titles and tags from your connected compatible comics site. If no site is connected, it waits for a connection. Filling runs while the app is in use and can be paused from its notice. Large libraries may use mobile data and battery.

Explore your reading tastes
Calculate taste statistics on your device from saved and opened works, and find related works. You can pause new analysis or reset its records without removing saved works.

Before you begin
• No websites or works are included by default. Enter the address of a supported site to start.
• Browsing and fetching work information require an internet connection. Features vary with each site's availability, account requirements and restrictions.
• Saving library entries does not create a complete offline media archive or sync favorites with website accounts.
• Translation sends images to an installed, supported external app. If direct sharing is unavailable, save the image and open it in that app. Availability depends on installation, supported features and network access. TEGNAL does not provide its own text-recognition or translation engine.
• In-app cloud library sync is currently unavailable.
• Follow the connected site's terms and access only content you are permitted to use. TEGNAL is not the official app of connected sites.
